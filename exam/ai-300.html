About the course

What AI-300 actually covers

The operations exam for AI, and the one that exists because demos are easy and production is not. Model lifecycle and operations carry the largest share, with GenAIOps infrastructure close behind, then the MLOps platform itself. The last quarter is quality assurance, observability and optimisation — proving a system still works, and making it cheaper while it does.

Assumed knowledge: Azure experience plus real machine learning or data science background. This is not an entry point.

The problems it answers

Why people in this role end up sitting AI-300

  1. A model that performed well in a notebook and has never been deployed anywhere.
  2. Retraining done by hand, by one person, whenever somebody notices drift.
  3. Generative systems with no evaluation harness, so quality is judged by anecdote.
  4. Inference costs that rise quietly until finance asks what changed.
  5. No observability on an AI system, so the first sign of failure is a complaint.
For training partners

Selling AI-300

Everything a partner needs to put this course in front of the right customer. Lift any of it straight into a proposal.

“They have data scientists who can build models and nobody who can run them. That gap is what this course is.”

When to bring it up

  • Models built and never deployed, or deployed once and never updated.
  • A generative AI feature in production with no evaluation or monitoring.
  • Inference costs rising with no owner.
  • A data science team being asked for SLAs they cannot currently meet.
  • An AI governance or audit requirement landing on an unprepared team.

Who signs: Head of data or platform engineering lead. Often funded from an AI programme rather than training.
Who attends: Machine learning engineers, MLOps and platform engineers, and data scientists moving towards production.

What to sell next

  • AI-103 for the developers building the applications
  • AI-500 when one agent becomes several
  • AZ-400 for the pipeline discipline underneath it
  • SC-500 once the AI workloads need securing

Pushbacks you will hear

And a straight answer for each

Our data scientists already deploy models.

Deploying once is not operating. Retraining, drift, evaluation and cost control are where this course lives, and they are most of the exam.

We only do generative AI, not classic ML.

Then roughly half the exam is directly yours — GenAIOps, evaluation and optimisation. Worth checking the split against their work before quoting.

Is this not the same as AI-103?

No. AI-103 builds the application. This one keeps it alive, measured and affordable afterwards.

Our models are not that critical.

Then start with the observability half. The argument gets easier the first time one silently degrades.
