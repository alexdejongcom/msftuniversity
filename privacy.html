Privacy

Privacy statement

What we collect, why, and how to make us forget it. Short version: as little as possible.

Version: 18 November 2026

Who we are

This website is run by alexdejong.com, a sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak) under Dutch law trading as Microsoft University, registered in Bergeijk, the Netherlands, Chamber of Commerce (KvK) number 17175279. We are the controller for the personal data described below. Questions or requests: alex@alexdejong.com.

The Redmond Report (newsletter)

  • What we store: your email address, when you signed up and confirmed, and a random code that makes the confirm and unsubscribe links work.
  • Why: to send you The Redmond Report, every two weeks. Nothing else.
  • Legal basis: your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), given by signing up and clicking the confirmation link. You can withdraw it at any time with the unsubscribe link in every issue.
  • How long: until you unsubscribe — we then delete your address immediately. Sign-ups that are never confirmed are deleted after 7 days.
  • No tracking: our mails contain no tracking pixels and no rewritten links. We don’t know whether you opened an issue or what you clicked.
  • Who processes it: the list is stored in Microsoft Azure Table Storage and mails are sent with Azure Communication Services, under Microsoft’s data processing terms. We never sell or share your address.

Certificates of attendance

When a certificate of attendance is generated during one of our courses, we register the attendee’s name, the course and the certificate ID, so the certificate can be verified later. The public verification page only shows the course and issue date — never the name.

Website statistics

We count visits with a single site-wide counter, and count visitors per country based on the time zone your browser reports. We don’t store IP addresses, don’t use cookies for this, and keep no per-visitor records.

Stored on your device only

A few preferences live in your browser’s local storage and never leave your device: unlocked achievements, Vista mode, and whether you dismissed the “install app” banner. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Content from other services

Some pages load small pieces of content from other services, which then receive your IP address like any website you visit: company logos on the events page come from Google’s favicon service, and the travel globe on the About page loads map data from jsDelivr. Links to Microsoft, YouTube, LinkedIn and others take you to their sites and their privacy policies.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its use, or to receive it in a portable format. Email alex@alexdejong.com and we’ll respond within a month. If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can also complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Changes

If this statement changes, we update the version date above. Material changes to how we use newsletter data are announced in The Redmond Report first.
